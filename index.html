About

I'm Andrei — a creative technologist based in Auckland, New Zealand.

I like the space where design, motion, and code overlap: figuring out how something should look and feel, then actually building it.

Alongside that, I'm completing a BSc in Computer Science & Statistics at the University of Auckland, which is where the more analytical, structured side of how I work comes from.

When I'm not writing code or cutting footage, I'm usually out shooting photos or chasing good light.
